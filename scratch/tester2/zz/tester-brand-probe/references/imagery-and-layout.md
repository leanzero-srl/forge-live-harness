# Imagery, resonance and layout

## The resonance system

The heart of the identity, and the thing that makes a layout read as diconium rather than as a
generic orange deck. "Resonance" means echo or reverberation — empathy causing things to vibrate in
sympathy. It shows up three ways.

**The symbol** — the diconium "D" echoed into decaying arcs, a reverberating mark. It can be cropped
by the format and used as a framing device, not only as a logo.

**The resonance grid** — vertical columns at proportional widths **¼X · X · X · X · 2X**. Use it to
structure editorial layouts and to crop imagery.

**The resonance image filter** — vertical line and glitch striping over photography, usually with a
warm-orange to cool-teal colour wash. Reserved for data, digital and AI subjects.

**Never apply the resonance filter to cars, or to client imagery.**

## Imagery categories

**Diverse experts** — real employee portraits, roughly **80% black and white, 20% colour**. The ratio
inverts to mostly colour for employer branding. A subtle light texture may be added but **never
between the subject and the viewer**. No stock photography.

**The big picture** — large-scale, impact, global reach. Colour or black and white.

**Clients** — the client's own product or headquarters imagery, used **unedited and never filtered**.

**Data and abstract** — abstract or detail shots through the resonance filter, in black and white,
natural colour, or orange combined with purple or blue.

Overall colour vibe: warm orange highlights against cool teal and blue shadows, high contrast.

## Form and surface

Geometric and sharp. **Default corner radius is 0** — a 2 to 4px softening exists but is secondary.
Surfaces are flat. Depth is restrained: prefer line and colour steps over shadows and glows.

**Avoid gradients as decoration.** The one sanctioned gradient is the warm-to-cool resonance wash.

Border widths: 1px thin, 2px medium, 3px thick. Focus ring is a 2px orange outline.

## Layout and spacing

White space is active and intentional — the guide's words are "calmness and professionalism". White
carries roughly 70% of a layout.

Build on an **8px grid**. The spacing scale is 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128px. Maximum
content container is 1200px.

Use the resonance column ratios for editorial layouts and image crops rather than an even grid.

## Motion

Calm and professional. Short fades and clean transitions, 120–400ms, standard easing. **No bounces**
and no playful overshoot.

Hover darkens orange to `#E64800`, or fills an outline to solid. Press darkens further to `#CC4000`.
No scale-shrink on press for buttons. Cards lift about 2px on hover.

## Icons

diconium ships **no proprietary icon set**. The identity carries through the symbol, the resonance
grid, CAPS typography and photography rather than spot icons.

The one recurring glyph is the arrow **→** on "read more" and "learn more" actions, set in the brand
font rather than an icon font.

If a piece genuinely needs functional icons, use a thin geometric outline set that matches Campton's
character — around 1.75px stroke, square joins, monochrome in black or orange for an active state.
Avoid filled and duotone styles. Treat this as a documented substitution and say so, because it is
not part of the official brand.

No emoji and no decorative unicode glyphs as iconography.
