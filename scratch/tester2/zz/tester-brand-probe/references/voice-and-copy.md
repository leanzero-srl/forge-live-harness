# Voice and copy

## Tone

Confident, forward-looking, plain-spoken thought leadership. Optimistic but precise. The brand
positions itself as a thought leader that "updates industries".

Avoid hype words. Favour concrete value — "more efficient, profitable, competitive" rather than
"revolutionary" or "cutting-edge". Where a claim can be made with a number, use the number.

## Person

**"we"** for diconium. **"you" / "your"** for the client. The register is collective and
partnership-oriented — "collective competence", "experts with empathy".

## Signature lines

Use these verbatim when they fit. Do not paraphrase them.

**Tagline** — "Updating industries". It is a message, not a lock-up element: it appears on covers
and on the website, and it **never sits under the logo**.

**One-liner** — "diconium is a global group building software delivery organizations."

**Value proposition** — "We navigate complexity and deliver competitiveness at scale."

**Rhetorical hook**, recurring — "future businesses don't need to be more digital. they need to be
smarter."

## The why / how / what framework

**Why** — updating industries and societies to build desirable futures.
**How** — empathy, diverse teams of experts, value creation.
**What** — we build software delivery organizations.

## Proof points

Used as bold numbers, set large. 2,500 experts. 30 years. 100% Volkswagen Group subsidiary. Present
in 4 of the 5 leading economies. Founded 1995. Around 2,500 experts across hubs in 6 countries.

diconium is a global digital business-transformation group, a wholly owned subsidiary of the
Volkswagen Group, generating value from software, data and AI, with a strong e-commerce heritage.

Named client success stories that appear in the company presentation: STIHL, MANN+HUMMEL, Bechtle,
Endress+Hauser, Kodak Alaris, TRUMPF.

## Hard rules

**No emoji.** Not part of the brand voice, in any medium.

**"diconium" is always lowercase**, including at the start of a sentence. See the casing reference.

Do not invent statistics or client names. If a number is needed and not listed here, ask for it
rather than producing a plausible one — these appear in client-facing material.
