# Colours

Source: diconium Design Guide 2024, "Colors", p.44–47.

## Brand core

| Token | Hex | RGB | Pantone | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| orange | `#FF5000` | 255, 80, 0 | Orange 021 C | Hero accent |
| white | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | — | Dominant base, ~70% of a layout |
| black | `#000000` | 0, 0, 0 | — | Mainly text |
| purple | `#581F9D` | 88, 31, 157 | 267 C | Secondary, short highlights and quotes |
| blue | `#003E96` | 0, 62, 150 | 2146 C | **Phase 2 — not in use, do not apply** |

## Resonance steps

Solid tints on white, not opacity. The colour-step system is part of the identity.

**Orange** — `#FF5000` (100) · `#FF7333` (80) · `#FF9666` (60) · `#FFB999` (40) · `#FFDCCC` (20) · `#FFF1EA` (08, faint wash for fills and hover surfaces)

**Purple** — `#581F9D` (100) · `#794CB1` (80) · `#9B79C4` (60) · `#BCA5D8` (40) · `#DED2EB` (20)

**Blue, phase 2, unused** — `#003E96` (100) · `#3365AB` (80) · `#668BC0` (60) · `#99B2D5` (40) · `#CCD8EA` (20)

## Greyscale

`#000000` · `#1A1A1A` (90) · `#333333` (80) · `#666666` (60) · `#999999` (40) · `#CCCCCC` (20) · `#E6E6E6` (10) · `#F2F2F2` (05) · `#FFFFFF`

## Semantic roles

Accent is orange. Accent hover darkens to `#E64800`, pressed to `#CC4000`. Secondary accent is purple.

Background is white; subtle background is `#F2F2F2`; inverse background is black. Body text is black,
muted text is `#666666`, inverse text is white, text on the accent is white.

Borders are `#CCCCCC`, strong borders black. Focus ring is a 2px orange outline.

Functional states stay inside the palette where possible: success `#1F8A5B`, warning is the brand
orange, danger `#C72A1C`, info is the phase-2 blue (so in practice avoid it until blue is live).

## Using them

White carries the layout. Orange is an accent and loses its force the moment it becomes a field —
a full orange slide is off-brand. Purple is for short highlights, quotes and calls to action, not
for body text or large areas. Balance is judged across a set of slides, not one at a time.
